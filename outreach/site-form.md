сайт лучше». Это текст анкеты. Пока отправляем его письмом (ответ — в том же письме или
файлы вложением); позже можно перенести в Google Forms. Отвечать можно коротко, любой

**Subject:** A few questions for your new website

Hi,

Thanks for replying! Answer whatever you like below, short is fine, and skip anything
you don't care about. We'll take the rest from your current site and Google listing.

1. **Name and logo.** Is the business name on the example right? Please attach your logo
   if you have one (any format, even a photo of a sign).
2. **Photos.** Attach a few photos you like: your place, your team, happy pets (with
   owners' OK). We replace all illustrations with them.
3. **Services and prices.** Which services should be on the site, and do you want prices
   shown? Paste your price list or attach a photo of it.
4. **What makes you different.** In one or two sentences, why do clients choose you?
5. **Team.** Names and a line about each person you'd like to introduce (optional).
6. **Booking.** How should new clients reach you: text, call, email, an online booking
   link (Square, MoeGo, Vagaro...)? Which phone number takes texts?
7. **Hours and area.** Are the hours on the example right? For mobile and sitting
   services: which neighborhoods or ZIP codes do you cover?
8. **Languages.** Should the site also be in Spanish (or another language)?
9. **Domain.** Keep your current domain, or do you need a new one? (A new domain is
   included in the $200.)
10. **Anything else** you'd like to add, remove or change.

As soon as we have your answers we'll update the example and send you the new link. You
pay only once the site is live on your domain and you're happy with it.
