# Message templates (US)

Placeholders: `{{owner}}` (first name if known, else "there"), `{{name}}`,
`{{rating}}`, `{{reviews}}`, `{{praise}}` (one thing customers praise, from
the site's highlights), `{{preview_url}}`, `{{suggested_domain}}` (a free .com we checked, e.g.
yofrengrooming.com), `{{price}}`, `{{sender}}`,
`{{company}}`, `{{postal_address}}`.

Keep it plain text, one link, under 120 words. Send by hand.

---

## Email 1 (day 0)

**Subject:** Built a website for {{name}}

Hi {{owner}},

{{rating}} stars from {{reviews}} Google reviews is impressive. Your customers keep mentioning {{praise}}.

I noticed {{name}} doesn't have a website, so I built one for you with your hours, services and reviews:

{{preview_url}}

If you like it, it can be live on your own domain (like {{suggested_domain}}) within 48 hours. {{price}} one-time, domain and hosting for the first year included. You only pay once it's live and you're happy with it.

Just reply "yes" and I'll set it up.

{{sender}}
{{company}} · We build websites for local pet businesses
{{postal_address}}
Not interested? Reply "stop" and I won't write again.
