Questions people ask

How much is a visit?

Visits are typically $26 for 30 minutes, plus $2 for each additional cat. Medications, injections, SQ fluids and inhalers are added per visit.

What's included in a visit?

Quality time with your cats, feeding, fresh water, scooping litter, bringing in mail and packages, and watering up to three indoor plants.

Can you give medications and fluids?

Yes: oral and transdermal medications, insulin, Adequan and B12 injections, SQ fluids and inhaler treatments.
