Questions people ask

Do you groom cats?

Yes. A cat bath is $85 for short hair and $95 for long hair, a groom without a bath is $110, and a full groom with a bath is $120.

How do I book?

Book online any time, or call us at 312-826-8794.

How do you size dogs?

By weight: small up to 20 lb, medium 21 to 40 lb, large 41 to 60 lb, and X-large 61 lb and up.

Why do some prices have a plus?

Doodle and poodle cuts, and full grooms for large dogs, depend on the breed, size and the condition of the coat.

Can my dog just get a nail trim?

Yes. A nail trim and grind is $20, and a nail trim with ear cleaning is $10 for dogs and $20 for cats.
