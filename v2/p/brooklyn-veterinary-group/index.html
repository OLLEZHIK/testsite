What clients say

Praise for attentive doctors and supportive technicians

Clients consistently commend doctors like Dr. Gambino, Dr. Dodd, and Dr. Pernice for their thorough examinations, clinical empathy, and dedication to patient care. Veterinary technicians and front-desk personnel receive warm appreciation, although a lone review notes an unwelcoming reception interaction upon arrival.

Compassionate approach to nervous and senior pets

Pet parents highlight the staff's gentle handling of fragile seniors and aggressive or fearful animals. Reviewers describe how technicians successfully calm anxious pets during imaging and examinations, showing genuine affection and treating every visiting dog and cat like their own family member.

Clear consultations and responsive medical guidance

Communication receives high praise across client feedback. Veterinarians dedicate generous time to answering owner questions about diet, preventative vaccines, and ongoing treatments, with pet parents noting fast email replies and thorough explanations regarding complex health issues.
