What clients say

4.7 on Google from 429 reviews

Dedicated clinicians with warm bedside manner

Clients express strong appreciation for the warmth, dedication, and clinical bedside manner of the veterinarians and support staff. Reviewers frequently single out Dr. Kudman and Dr. Q for their empathetic approach and thorough explanations during routine visits as well as emotional end-of-life care. One critical review noted a negative interaction regarding an ailing kitten, but the vast majority describe a supportive team.

Patient and comforting pet handling

Pet owners consistently praise the gentle, considerate handling their dogs and cats receive during appointments. Several clients note that nervous or rescue pets were kept relaxed and comfortable throughout physical examinations, blood work, and routine vaccinations. Reviewers emphasize that the veterinarians treat each animal with patience and genuine care, never rushing through exams.
