What clients say

4.6 on Google from 326 reviews

Crisp breed trims and refreshing baths

Clients consistently praise the high visual quality of grooming services, describing dogs and long-haired cats coming home neatly styled, smelling fresh, and free of tangled mats. While two reviewers complained of uneven trims and long wait times, most praise the skillful scissor work.

Gentle handling for puppies and dogs

Pet owners appreciate the patient care extended to first-time puppies, nervous rescues, and large breeds such as Samoyeds and Great Pyrenees. Most pets leave feeling relaxed after spa treatments, though one client reported a severe nail and anal gland issue that required veterinary care.

Friendly grooming crew with billing disputes

Groomers Emily, Maria, and Carolina receive frequent praise for their courteous communication, attentive styling notes, and warm demeanor. A few dissenting reviews mention price discrepancies at pickup and long waiting times, but the overwhelming consensus highlights caring customer service.
