What clients say

4.9 on Google from 388 reviews

Dedicated and communicative veterinary doctors

Pet owners speak very highly of Dr Eric Matkowski and Dr Sarah Organist, describing them as empathetic and exceptionally thorough. Reviewers frequently note that the doctors answer questions in detail and listen carefully to owner concerns. With only minor remarks about busy reception periods, the team is widely regarded as thoughtful and dependable.

Special patience for reactive dogs

Multiple clients highlight the practice's ability to handle nervous, reactive, or large dogs with calm expertise. Reviewers mention that staff take the time required for anxious pets to settle down, ensuring exams proceed without stress. Pet owners feel immense relief knowing their sensitive rescue animals are handled with genuine gentleness.

Thorough diagnostic and medical care

Clients report strong satisfaction with ongoing treatment plans for chronic conditions, rescue checkups, and wellness examinations. While one reviewer felt that unnecessary medications were recommended, the vast majority praise accurate assessments and transparent medical guidance. Pet owners credit the clinic with keeping their animals healthy.
