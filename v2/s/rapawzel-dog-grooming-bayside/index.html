What clients say

4.9 on Google from 900 reviews

Praise for gentle and accommodating groomers

Clients frequently commend the individual groomers for their punctuality, attentiveness, and friendly communication during appointments. Multiple pet parents appreciate that groomers listen closely to styling requests and handle energetic dogs with patience, though one customer experienced frustration over sudden scheduling changes.

Neat haircuts and fresh-smelling coats

Dog owners report that their pets return looking tidy, smelling clean, and sporting well-shaped coats after grooming sessions. Many reviewers highlight precise breed cuts and pleasant finishing touches like bandanas. However, one owner was upset when a double-coated dog was unexpectedly shaved down.

Calm handling for sensitive dogs

Pet parents highlight the gentle care given to senior and anxious dogs during mobile and salon appointments. Reviewers note that groomers work calmly to keep pets relaxed throughout nail trims and baths. Aside from an isolated complaint about a shaved coat, owners feel confident leaving their companions in safe hands.
