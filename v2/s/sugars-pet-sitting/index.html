What clients say

5.0 on Google from 72 reviews

Dependable and patient sitting team

Pet parents describe the sitters as trustworthy, kind, and willing to go the extra mile, including navigating tricky entry logistics or making visits during harsh winter weather. Clients note that regular caregivers build strong relationships with household pets, following specific feeding routines and care directions thoroughly.

Attentive companionship for dogs and cats

Owners frequently share that both dogs and cats appear relaxed, joyful, and well settled upon their return from work trips or family holidays. Reviewers appreciate the gentle handling provided to nervous rescues, older cats, and large multi-dog households, noting that animals eagerly greet their sitters whenever they arrive at the door.

Prompt updates with detailed journals

Clients consistently praise the frequent text updates, detailed visit summaries, and daily photographs sent after each drop-in appointment. Pet owners express great reassurance knowing exactly how their companions are doing while away, highlighting prompt communication and quick answers to questions from the administrative team.
