What clients say

Attentive and dependable local walkers

Clients in Bushwick and Bed-Stuy enthusiastically recommend founder Bertlee and team members like Brian, Andy, Quinn, and Kei. Pet owners praise the walkers for their natural connection with animals, punctuality, and willingness to assist with unexpected home mishaps while clients are away.

Personalized care for special-needs pups

Reviewers emphasize how thoughtfully walkers handle three-legged dogs, reactive rescue pit bulls, senior pets, and dogs that feel anxious around strangers. Walkers take time for slow introductions and structured pack outings that help cautious dogs gain comfort and sociability.

Engaging walk logs and photo updates

Dog parents appreciate the timely notifications received after every scheduled outing through the client portal. Each update features expressive photographs, playful recaps, and notes on how pets felt during their adventures, giving owners complete peace of mind throughout the workday.
