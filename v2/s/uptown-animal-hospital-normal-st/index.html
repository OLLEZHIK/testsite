What clients say

4.6 on Google from 292 reviews

Devoted and welcoming veterinary personnel

Pet owners consistently praise Dr Romero, Dr Juan Pablo, Dr Hasak, and the nursing assistants for their warm, professional, and patient approach. Reviewers appreciate that veterinary surgeons explain diagnostic options clearly and remember pets by name. While one visitor noted an unresolved service issue, the overwhelming majority describe a team that delivers exemplary support.

Low-stress care for reactive pets

Families highlight the hospital's fear-free philosophy, remarking on how easily reactive dogs and carrier-stressed cats relax during consultations. Clients appreciate that veterinary nurses offer treats, soothing words, and gentle restraint during routine check-ups and blood draws. Pet owners express immense relief finding a practice where pets feel genuinely secure.

Fear-free clinic environment and accessible facilities

Clients appreciate the hospital's dedicated fear-free setting, which helps nervous dogs and anxious cats settle down before examinations begin. Multiple reviewers praise the convenient neighbourhood location and dedicated on-site parking facilities. While nearby street renovations can occasionally complicate access, pet owners consider the facility well arranged and easy to visit.
