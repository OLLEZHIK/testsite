What clients say

4.7 on Google from 314 reviews

Caring and knowledgeable veterinary doctors

Clients consistently commend Dr. Ori, Dr. Jordan, Dr. Laura, and their nursing staff for their thoroughness, patience, and empathetic approach. Reviewers note that the team explains diagnostic tests and treatment options in detail without making owners feel rushed. While most feedback is glowing, a couple of clients felt let down during emergency situations or unresolved conditions.

Gentle handling for anxious companions

Pet owners emphasize how comfortable their dogs and cats feel at this Brickell Key facility. Reviewers share that veterinary assistants and doctors take special care with nervous or reactive pets, sitting on the floor and offering treats during checkups. Many clients report that pets who normally dread clinic visits remain relaxed and happy throughout examinations.

Clear explanations and travel documentation

Reviewers praise the clinic team for attentive and transparent communication regarding medical findings, treatment plans, and international pet travel documentation. Clients appreciate that veterinarians dedicate ample time to answer questions over the phone and in person, providing reassurance and guiding owners through complex health certificate requirements.
