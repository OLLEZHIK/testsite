What clients say

Compassionate doctors and veterinary team

Clients speak warmly of Dr Yard and the veterinary assistants, highlighting their thorough medical knowledge and gentle empathy during difficult appointments. Reviewers appreciate that doctors take time to sit on the floor with nervous dogs and provide reassuring explanations. Despite isolated criticism of front desk receptionists, veterinary staff receive widespread commendation from pet owners.

Gentle approach towards anxious animals

Pet owners appreciate the patient handling shown towards anxious rescues, foster pets, and senior animals undergoing examination. Reviewers recount that veterinary clinicians move gently to keep dogs and cats calm, minimising stress throughout procedures such as nail clipping or lump evaluations. Many clients express lasting gratitude for the sensitive bedside manner shown to their companions.
