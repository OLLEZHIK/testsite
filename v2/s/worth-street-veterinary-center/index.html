What clients say

4.8 on Google from 449 reviews

Dedicated doctors and empathetic veterinary technicians

Clients praise Dr. Green, Dr. DiPolo, Dr. Boulting, and Dr. Stern for combining thorough medical expertise with genuine empathy for anxious pets and owners. Multiple reviewers mention that veterinarians take unhurried time to review treatment choices. A few reviewers noted feelings of reduced personal connection over time or frustration during isolated visits.

Responsive messaging and thorough explanations

Pet parents highlight excellent communication through email and text help lines, noting that clinical staff answer pre-visit questions promptly. Veterinarians receive praise for walking through complex treatment paths in plain terms. One client, however, experienced frustration trying to obtain diagnostic lab results when a treating doctor worked on a part-time schedule.
