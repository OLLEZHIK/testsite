Questions people ask

Where does the grooming happen?

In our clean, fully equipped mobile grooming salon, parked at your home.

How much does a groom cost?

It depends on your pet's size, breed and coat condition. Send us the details and we'll tell you.

Do you groom cats?

Yes: a Cat Wash or a Cat Complete with a haircut.

What if I need to cancel?

Please let us know ahead of time. Cancelling within the hour of the appointment, or a no-show, adds a $10 fee to the next visit.

How can I pay?

Cash or credit card: Visa, MasterCard and Discover.
