Questions people ask

Why mobile grooming?

Your dog is groomed one-on-one, without the stress of a kennel and away from other dogs, which also lowers the risk of picking up illnesses from group settings.

What's in a bath?

Eco-friendly products, brushing for the coat, face and body shampoo, nail trim, paw and sanitary trim, ear cleaning, and anal glands if you'd like.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your dog's size, coat and the service. Send a text with the details for a price. Longer distances from Uptown cost extra.

When can you come?

Every day from 10 am to 10 pm.
